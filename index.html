Are Flavapp apps free?
Yes. All our apps are free to download on Google Play. They are supported by ads, and some apps offer an optional in-app purchase to remove ads.
Which devices are supported?
Our apps are built for Android phones and tablets. TV, car and USB features depend on your TV, car display, cables and Android version, so compatibility can vary from one setup to another.
Do you sell my personal data?
No. We are not in the business of selling personal data. Ads and analytics are provided by Google services. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Where can I read each app's terms?
Every app has its own Terms of Use page. You can find all of them on our Terms of Use hub.
How do I get help with an app?
Send us a message from the Contact page or write to info@flavapp.online. Tell us the app name and your device model so we can help faster.
